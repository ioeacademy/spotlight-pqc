Seven blocks make one tunnel

A Cisco IKEv2 VPN is a chain of small configuration blocks. Each block has a job, and blocks point to each other by name. Build R1 and R3 side by side: a row is one kind of block, so you compare the two routers by reading across.

  • =Must match on both routers: algorithms, key, transform set.
  • ⇄Must mirror: my address is your peer address.
  • localLocal only: names are labels inside one router. They don't have to match the peer.

On every block, ▾ collapses or expands its fields and 👁 shows the IOS it becomes. Collapse a block and keep 👁 on to read it as configuration only.
